    How sure are we?

    The land here is today’s coastlines, moved to where a scientific model says they sat. The real shorelines were different: shallow seas often covered land that’s dry now. The further back you go, the less certain the map gets.

    “Near you” means where fossils were found, measured on today’s map. It only covers the 19 dinosaurs on this site, so plenty of others lived near you too. Where the data comes from.
