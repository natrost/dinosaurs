One call per dinosaur, named by its id in `js/data.js`. Parasaurolophus has no clip: its crest horn is made in `js/sound.js`.

Made from three National Park Service recordings (public domain, please credit NPS), cleaned up, slowed down and layered:
- American alligator, Everglades (`nri-AlligatorEVER1.mp3`)
- Bison, Yellowstone (`nri-BisonYELL.mp3`)
- Common raven, Grand Teton (`nri-CommonRavenGRTE.mp3`)

Source: https://www.nps.gov/subjects/sound/gallery.htm

These are best guesses. For a new dinosaur, add its clip here and an entry in `CALLS` in `js/sound.js`.
